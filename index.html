About DMD Motion

DMD Motion LLC is a performance vehicle dealership located in Bertram, Texas — just 35 minutes northwest of Austin. We specialize in hand-picked trucks, muscle cars, and 4x4s that are built to perform.

Every vehicle in our inventory is personally inspected and selected. We don't do volume — we focus on quality units that we'd drive ourselves. When you buy from DMD Motion, you're getting a vehicle we stand behind.

Hill Country Location

Located in Bertram, TX on the edge of the Texas Hill Country. Easy access from Austin, Georgetown, Marble Falls, and Lampasas.

No Hassle Deals

Straightforward pricing with no hidden fees. What you see is what you pay. We'll work with you to get the deal done right.

Quality Over Quantity

We keep a small, curated inventory of vehicles we've personally vetted. Every truck, car, and SUV meets our standards before it hits the lot.

Financing

DMD Motion works with multiple lenders to help you find financing that fits your budget. Whether you have great credit, are rebuilding, or are a first-time buyer, we'll work to get you approved.

Flexible Options

Competitive rates through our network of lending partners. We shop multiple lenders to find you the best rate available.

Trade-Ins Accepted

Have a vehicle to trade? We'll give you a fair offer and apply it toward your purchase. Bring it by for a free appraisal.
